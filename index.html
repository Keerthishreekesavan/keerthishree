    ISED-2025

    13th IEEE International Conference on Intelligent Systems and Embedded Design · NIT Raipur

    Hybrid SVM–Naïve Bayes Ensemble with LIME + SHAP for Transparent Air Quality & Health Risk Prediction

    Keerthishree Kesavan and co-authors

    Sri Ramachandra Institute of Higher Education and Research, Chennai

    Abstract, A hybrid ensemble that combines Support Vector Machines with Gaussian Naïve Bayes to predict air quality (AQI) and multi-disease health risk. LIME and SHAP explain every prediction, so the model is transparent rather than a black box, and an interactive geospatial dashboard visualises the results in real time. The model reaches 96.2% AQI accuracy with an F1-score of 0.921.

    Index Terms, air quality index, ensemble learning, SVM, Gaussian Naïve Bayes, explainable AI, LIME, SHAP, geospatial visualisation.
